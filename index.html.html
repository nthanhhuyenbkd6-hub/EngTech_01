    35–60 min · Skimming

    Skim to find the main idea

    Skimming means you read fast and look for the big idea. Look at the title and the first sentence of each part.

    How a Tablet Works

    A tablet is a small device with a big screen. It is flat and light, so you can carry it in a bag. Many people use tablets at home and at school. You can draw, read and play games on it. You do not need a keyboard or a mouse to use a tablet. You tap the screen with your finger, and the tablet does what you ask.

    Many tablets also have a small button on the side. You press it to start the tablet. You press it again to make the screen dark. The button is small, but it is very useful.

    Inside the tablet, there is a battery. The battery gives the tablet power, so you can use it without a charger. When the battery is low, you use the charger. A tablet needs some time to charge. Do not wait until the battery is empty.

    A tablet can connect to Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi lets the tablet use the internet. With Wi-Fi, you can watch videos, find answers and talk to friends. Without Wi-Fi, you can still play some games, but many things do not work.

    Sometimes you need to type a message. The tablet shows a keyboard on the screen. You tap the letters, and the words appear. You type with your fingers, but you do not press real keys. You can also connect a real keyboard to the tablet. Then you can type faster. You can connect a mouse too.

    Keep your tablet safe. Hold it with two hands, and keep it away from water. Do not put food or drinks near the screen. A clean screen is easy to see and easy to tap. A tablet is a useful device, but you need to do other things too. Play outside and talk with your family.

    Questions

    1. 1True or false? You need a mouse to use a tablet. Correct the sentence if it is false.

      False. You tap the screen, so you do not need a mouse.

    2. 2True or false? The battery gives the tablet power. Correct the sentence if it is false.

      True.

    3. 3What do you use when the battery is low?

      The charger.

    4. 4What does Wi-Fi let a tablet use?

      The internet.
